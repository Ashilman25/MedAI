Title: Hypertension — Guideline Summary (Adults)
URL: https://www.heart.org/
Summary: Confirm diagnosis with repeated measurements or ABPM/HBPM. Initial therapy options: thiazide‑type diuretic, ACE inhibitor, ARB, or CCB. Target BP generally <130/80 mmHg for most high‑risk adults. Counsel on sodium restriction, weight reduction, physical activity, moderation of alcohol, and DASH diet. Reassess every 4–6 weeks and titrate to goal. Watch for secondary causes when resistant.
