Title: Pediatric Fever — Initial Assessment
URL: https://www.aap.org/
Summary: Use age‑appropriate thresholds; neonates with fever require prompt evaluation. Identify red flags: poor perfusion, respiratory distress, altered mental status, petechiae. Ensure hydration and antipyretics for comfort (acetaminophen/ibuprofen dosing by weight). Consider source‑directed testing (urine, respiratory). Provide clear return precautions to caregivers.
