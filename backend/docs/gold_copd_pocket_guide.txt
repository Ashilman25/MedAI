Title: COPD — GOLD Pocket Guide Highlights
URL: https://goldcopd.org/
Summary: Diagnosis requires spirometry (post‑bronchodilator FEV1/FVC <0.70). Assess symptoms (mMRC/CAT) and exacerbation risk to guide therapy. First‑line pharmacotherapy typically includes bronchodilators (LAMA/LABA). Consider inhaled corticosteroids in patients with frequent exacerbations and eosinophils ≥300/µL or concomitant asthma. Non‑pharmacologic care: smoking cessation, vaccines, pulmonary rehab. Exacerbations: short course oral steroids and antibiotics when indicated.
